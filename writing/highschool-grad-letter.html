End Of Year Letter

I gave this letter to my graduating class, written for them all.

By James Obergh May, 2024 5 minute read

Introduction

BHigh school is over; slipped between fingers and dropped in grates, like old quarters and dimes. There is so much to do! Lives to make! People to become!

Thank you for walking with me along our individual journeys. PCA was fun and free. There will always be a part of me that wishes I spoke more or connected more, but I think it’ll be alright. This was so good. Thank you all. I want to leave you some final words—though before that, here is some of my contact information in case you wish to reconnect or talk! I trust you guys!

A smaller supporting idea

For my final high school goodbye, I leave you with a poem from Lain Thomas, “The Light That Shines When Things End.”

Example

“ I hope that in the future they invent a small golden light that follows you everywhere and when something is about to end, it shines brightly so you know it's about to end. And if you're never going to see someone again, it'll shine brightly and both of you can be polite and say, "It was nice to have you in my life while I did, good luck with everything that happens after now." And maybe if you're never going to eat at the same restaurant again, it'll shine and you can order everything off the menu you've never tried. Maybe, if someone's about to buy your car, the light will shine and you can take it for one last spin. Maybe, if you're with a group of friends who'll never be together again, all your lights will shine at the same time and you'll know, and then you can hold each other and whisper, "This was so good. Oh my God, this was so good." “

Conclusion

Goodbye friends. So much to say, now so little time. Thank you!! Smile and run, catch the trade winds in your sails, and sail from safe shores and far harbors. Off to wonderful things, I hope. This was so good. Oh my god, this was so good.
